On-the-floor shoot sequence

Follow this order for every stall you cover.

Shoot in 4K, back camera only

Every clip goes to 4K resolution using the rear camera. No front-camera or downgraded-quality footage will be accepted.

Open with a viral-style hook

Start every reel like you've just spotted something India hasn't seen before. Sell the discovery in the first two seconds — see the example script below.

Talk to the stall owner on camera

Good communication is the job. Engage the owner directly, keep it conversational, and let their answers carry the middle of the video.

Ask real product questions

Pull out the specifications that make the product interesting — range, price, warranty, what makes it different. Good questions are what make the edit good.

Close on the contact clip

Every video ends with a clean shot of the owner pointing to or reading out the mobile number and address shown on their display. This clip is mandatory — our edit team overlays these details in post.

Example opening hook

"Are bhai, ye kya cheez laaye ho nayi! India mein aapne kabhi aisa nahi dekha hoga — aaj aapke liye laaye hain aisi unique cheez, dekhke aap aaj hi ghar le aayenge."

Use this as a tone reference, not a script to repeat word-for-word — react to what's actually in front of you at each stall.

FAQ

Do I need to edit the footage before sending it?

No. Send raw, unedited clips only. R&M's edit team handles cuts, captions, and adding the contact details overlay.

What if a stall doesn't want to be filmed?

Move on to the next one. Only film owners who are comfortable talking on camera — the interaction has to feel natural, not forced.

How many stalls should I cover?

Quality over quantity. A handful of well-shot, well-questioned stalls with clean contact clips beats a large number of rushed ones.

Can I post any of this to my own page?

No. All footage is exclusive to R&M. See the confidentiality & copyright section above — this is enforced.

What camera settings should I use?

4K resolution, rear camera, steady handheld or gimbal if you have one. Match the pacing and framing of the reference reels above.

Who do I contact if something comes up on shoot day?

Ping Naman bhai. For anything else, use the contact details below.
